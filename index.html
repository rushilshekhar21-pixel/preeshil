Our story

It is never too late to begin again.

Every bag carries more than what we put inside it. It carries a little of who we are, what we value, where we are going and the person we choose to be.

Preeshil was born from a simple belief: a new beginning does not belong to a particular age, stage or circumstance. It belongs to anyone willing to take the first step.

At the heart of Preeshil is a woman putting that belief into practice. Driven by her passion for bags, she is building a brand on her own terms.

Read the full story

Life rarely unfolds exactly as we imagine. Our priorities change, our paths take unexpected turns, and sometimes we get to ask a different question: what do I want the next chapter of my life to look like? Preeshil is about having the courage to answer it.

We believe a bag is deeply personal. It is an extension of your style, your choices and your individuality. We want every Preeshil bag to be thoughtfully made, dependable in everyday life, and distinctive enough to feel like your own.

Preeshil stands for possibility. For the woman returning to a long-held ambition. For the person discovering a new passion. For anyone who has been told that their moment has passed.

There is no prescribed age for ambition, no single definition of success and no limit to the possibilities we can create for ourselves.
